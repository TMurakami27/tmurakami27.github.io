Portfolio / Project

Bank funding through COVID-19, Hirotec Mexico

Sumitomo Corporation secondment · Guanajuato, Mexico · Feb 2020 – Dec 2021

Context

I was Assistant Manager on secondment to Hirotec Mexico, a car parts manufacturer and JV subsidiary of Sumitomo with $500M+ USD revenue, during the Covid-19 period (Feb 2020 – Dec 2021).

What I did

  • Refined cash flow projections, designed a new borrowing renewal framework, and negotiated with banks, leading a team of 6 Mexican colleagues and enabling them to contribute through value-adding tasks.
  • Worked on loan facilities with 4 banks after a bank reduced a credit line by $20M USD, due to manufacturing plant shutdowns related to Covid-19.

Result

  • Decreased interest payments by 50%.
  • Achieved $60M USD expansion of loan facilities from 4 banks, saving the company from bankruptcy and avoiding layoffs.
